Commission Fees

Fees are based on your 30-day trading volume. Pay in SATL or USDC — you choose.

How it works

  1. Your 30-day rolling trade volume is tracked automatically (in USD)
  2. Volume below $10 = no fees
  3. Once you exceed $10, fees apply to all subsequent trades within the 30-day window
  4. Fees are charged per trade, deducted from your GasStation balance
  5. Pay in SATL or USDC — select your preference in the GasStation panel

Trading Robots

Access to automated trading robots (Market Maker, Scalping) is paid in USDC on Base network.

Cost: 10 USDC / month per active robot
